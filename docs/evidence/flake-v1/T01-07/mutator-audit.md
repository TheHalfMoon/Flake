## Summary

- **1 genuine unclosed finding from `T00-02`'s own inventory** (`EventLog::open`'s `create_dir_all`) — found, root-caused, fixed, regression-tested. See `docs/evidence/flake-v1/T01-01/CORRECTIVE-ADDENDUM-T01-07.md`.
- **1 genuine gap in `T01-04`'s own verification logic** (`verify_recovery_candidate` never cross-checked `revision.payload` against its stored `payload_sha256`, nor the redundant `revision`/`command` fields) — found directly by this task's own D4 fault-schedule matrix (not by inspection alone), root-caused, fixed, and the fix's effectiveness re-proven by redesigning the matrix to eliminate a confound (SQLite freelist/dead-page bytes) that made the original matrix's floor unfalsifiable in either direction. See `REPORT.md` "Defects found and fixed during this task."
- **9 of 11 audited mutators were already correctly closed** by their owning tasks and are re-verified unchanged here.
- **No mutator was omitted from this audit because it was inconvenient.** Every `pub`/`pub(crate)` mutating function across `vault.rs`, `events.rs`, `canonical.rs`, `recovery.rs`, `backup.rs`, `migration.rs` is accounted for above or in Part 1's unchanged rows.
