Throwaway harness (deleted before commit; not part of the shipped diff):

  temp roots (release build), then 3x warm `CanonicalStore::open` on each of
  the 30 published stores (90 opens total).
- examples/zz_sqlite_version.rs: `SELECT sqlite_version()` on an in-memory
  connection opened by the same vendored (bundled) rusqlite/libsqlite3-sys

Interpretation (S-scale only; not a plan §27 M/L release measurement — see
REPORT.md "Performance gate" and docs/formats/format-2-canonical-sqlite.md
"Known limitations"):
- create (cold, staged-create-verify-publish) p95 38.3ms is well inside the
  closest plan §27 analog, "Save/write acknowledgement <=64 KiB": target
  p95 150ms, maximum 750ms.
- open (warm reopen with full pragma re-assertion + schema recognition)
  p95 1.1ms is well inside "Clean startup integrity quick checks M": target
  250ms, maximum 1s.
- Both are single-empty-store measurements (no records, no history), not a
  dataset-M/L measurement; those datasets do not exist until P02 introduces
  typed records.
